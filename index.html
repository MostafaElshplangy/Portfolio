About

I'm an IT and network engineer with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Menofia University, and hands-on CCNA training covering TCP/IP, routing, switching, VLANs, DHCP, DNS, and VPNs. My work is grounded in lab-tested practice — every protocol I list, I've configured, broken, and fixed myself in Cisco Packet Tracer and live training environments.

My approach to infrastructure is simple: reliability and security aren't add-ons, they're the design brief from the start. Whether it's segmenting traffic with VLANs, building redundancy with HSRP, or locking down remote access over SSH, I care about networks that stay up and stay safe under real conditions.

I'm currently expanding into cloud and DevOps through the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative, connecting traditional networking foundations to the infrastructure-as-code and cloud-native practices shaping where the field is heading.
